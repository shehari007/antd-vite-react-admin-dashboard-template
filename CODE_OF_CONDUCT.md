# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a good experience for everyone,
whatever their experience level, background, identity, or first language. This
is a template that many people will use for their first React project, and
beginners asking basic questions are exactly who it is for.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good faith
- Give feedback on the code, not the person
- Accept that people are working with different amounts of time and experience
- Ask before assuming someone made a careless mistake

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Dismissing or mocking a question because it is a beginner question

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this, email
**shehariyar@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately, and the person
reporting will not be named without their agreement.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and pull
requests that do not follow this document, and may temporarily or permanently
ban anyone for behaviour they judge to be harmful.
